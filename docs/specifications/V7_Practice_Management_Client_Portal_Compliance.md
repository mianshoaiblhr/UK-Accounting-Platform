# V7 — Practice Management / Client Portal / Compliance

## Objective
Make the platform the operating system for an accounting practice, not just an accounts engine.

## Practice Dashboard
Show:
- clients
- deadlines
- overdue tasks
- work in progress
- filings
- review queue
- client risk
- compliance health.

## Client Onboarding
- KYC/AML workflow
- company information
- directors
- beneficial owners
- tax registrations
- engagement
- risk assessment
- document collection.

## Compliance Calendar
Track:
- accounts
- corporation tax
- VAT
- PAYE-related tasks
- confirmation statement
- MTD obligations
- internal deadlines.

## Client Portal
Client can:
- upload documents
- view requests
- answer questions
- approve accounts
- approve tax returns
- view tasks
- receive secure messages
- see filing status.

## AI Client Request Engine
Create a single action list:
“3 items required from you.”

Prioritise by:
- deadline
- materiality
- risk
- dependency.

## Compliance Health Score
Example:
Accounts 100%
VAT 100%
CT 85%
Documents 70%
Bank 100%
Overall score 91%.

Score must be explainable and configurable.

## Client Communication
Templates, secure messaging, reminders and escalation.
