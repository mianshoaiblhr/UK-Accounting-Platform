## Objective
Move from historical accounting to decision intelligence.

## Forecasts
- 30-day
- 60-day
- 90-day
- 180-day

## Cash Flow

- collections
- supplier payments
- payroll
- VAT
- tax
- debt
- capex
- recurring costs.

## Scenarios
User can ask:
- What if revenue falls 10%?
- Can I afford a £50,000 vehicle?
- What if I hire 5 staff?
- What happens if debtor days increase?

## KPIs
- revenue growth
- gross margin
- EBITDA
- net margin
- debtor days
- creditor days
- cash conversion
- working capital
- break-even.

## Explain This Number
Every major metric must support:
metric → period comparison → drivers → ledger accounts → source transactions → documents.

## Management Packs
Generate:
- executive summary
- P&L
- balance sheet
- cash flow
- KPIs
- variance analysis
- risks
- recommendations.

## AI Boundary
Forecast assumptions must be visible and editable.
AI recommendations must be scenario-based, not presented as guaranteed outcomes.
