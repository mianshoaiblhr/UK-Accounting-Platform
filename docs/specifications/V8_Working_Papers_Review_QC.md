## Lead Schedules
Every balance sheet account can have a lead schedule with:
- opening balance
- movements
- closing balance
- supporting documents
- reconciliation
- reviewer comments.

## Review Workflow
PREPARED
→ SELF REVIEW
→ MANAGER REVIEW
→ PARTNER REVIEW
→ CLIENT APPROVAL
→ FILED.

## Review Points
Reviewers can:
- raise query
- assign query
- request evidence
- accept
- reject
- clear point.

## AI Year-End Review
Identify:
- unusual movements
- unreconciled balances
- possible accruals
- possible prepayments
- fixed asset candidates
- revenue cut-off
- director loan issues
- related party indicators
- unusual legal/professional costs
- suspense.

AI produces review points; accountant controls clearance.

## Quality Control
Support firm-level:
- mandatory procedures
- sampling
- review completion
- sign-off
- audit trail.
